Context

Highlight text anywhere, search it in the sites you actually use.

Getting started

  1. Add a destination. Click the Context icon in your toolbar to open the panel, go to Destinations, and press +. A destination is any site you search often.
    The URL template is the trick: run a search on the site, copy the resulting URL, and replace your search words with {term}.
    Googlehttps://www.google.com/search?q={term}
    Gmailhttps://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#search/{term}
    GitHubhttps://github.com/search?q={term}&type=issues
  2. Highlight text on any page — a name, an email address, a ticket ID.
  3. Search it, whichever way suits you:
    • Press ⌘M to open the panel with your highlighted text already filled in, then pick a destination.
    • Right-click → Context → pick a destination.
    • Press ⌥1, ⌥2, or ⌥3 to jump straight to one of your top destinations.

Context reuses an existing tab on the destination site when it can, so you don't end up with twelve Gmail tabs. Turn that off per destination with Always open a new tab.

Keyboard shortcuts

Chrome owns these bindings — that's why Context needs no access to the sites you visit. Quick-search slots map to your first five destinations, in the order they appear in the panel. On macOS, ⌘M may be reserved for window-minimize; rebind it if it won't take.
