Link-a-roo

Getting started

  1. Add a destination using the section below. A destination is any site you search often — press + Add, give it a label, and paste a URL template with {term} where the search words go.
  2. Highlight text on any page.
  3. Search whichever way suits you:
    • Press ⌘⇧S to open the search panel (any highlighted text will load in the search bar), then click or tab over to one of your destinations.
    • Right-click → Link-a-roo → pick a destination.
    • Use keyboard shortcuts to search highlighted text even faster.

Link-a-roo reuses an existing tab on the destination site when it can, so you don't end up with twelve Gmail tabs. Turn that off per destination with Always open a new tab.

Destinations

Tab reuse

Chrome wouldn't release this permission, so tab reuse is still on. That happens when the permission was granted by a version that required it. To clear it, remove Link-a-roo at chrome://extensions and load it again.

Link-a-roo needs permission to see your open tabs

To send a search to a tab you already have open, Link-a-roo has to check the addresses of your open tabs. Chrome will ask you to allow this next.

Nothing leaves your device. Link-a-roo has no server and sends no analytics — it reads the addresses only to find a tab to reuse, and keeps no record of them or of what you search.

New destination

Use {term} where the search text goes. Run a search on the site, copy the URL, and replace your search words with {term}.
